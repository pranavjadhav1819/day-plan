The Reset Blueprint

Break the loop first

Before the schedule and the workouts mean anything, the environment has to stop working against you. Three rules that make discipline the path of least resistance.

🚫

The Golden Rule

No phones in the bathroom or bed. Charge your phone across the room at night. If the physical barrier exists, 80% of accidental slips vanish.

🌙

Urge Surfing

A strong urge peaks like a wave for 10–15 minutes then drops. Name it — "I'm bored right now" — then switch environments immediately: a walk, a window, or 20 bodyweight squats.

🛡️

Digital Blocker

Install a blocker app on every device tonight. Make the loop harder to enter than it is to walk away from.

"Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most."
